## Quality Standards

When filling out this template, ensure:

- **Completeness:** Every section has specific content, no generic text left
- **Actionability:** Steps are detailed enough to execute without asking questions
- **Consistency:** Follows the project's existing patterns and conventions
- **Clarity:** Someone unfamiliar with the project could understand and execute the plan
- **Traceability:** Changes are connected back to goals and rationale
