## Twitter / X Guidelines

### Single Post (up to 280 chars)
- Open with a hook or relatable pain point
- One clear insight or value proposition
- Optional: 1 soft CTA
- Max 2-3 hashtags from the hashtag library

### Thread (5 tweets)
- Tweet 1: Hook or bold claim
- Tweets 2-3: Education or insight breakdown
- Tweet 4: How the product connects to the topic
- Tweet 5: Soft CTA or follow prompt

---

## Telegram Guidelines

- Length: 400 to 1200 characters
- More educational tone than Twitter, explain the why
- Use bullet points for clarity where appropriate
- Use line breaks generously, avoid walls of text
- End with an engagement question relevant to the audience
