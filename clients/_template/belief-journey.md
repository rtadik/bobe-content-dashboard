# {display_name} — Belief Journey

> Auto-generated during onboarding from intake data. Edit these belief stages to refine the education content strategy.
> Each stage represents a belief your target audience must hold before they are ready to become a customer.
> The pipeline uses these 7 stages to generate weekly Education bucket content topics.

---

## Belief Stage 1: Awareness of the Problem
**Belief needed:** "I have a real problem that is costing me something."
**Starting belief:** They may not yet clearly recognise the pain or its true cost.
**Content angle:** Surface the problem without selling. Make the audience feel seen and understood.
**Example topic title:** "Why [target audience] keep facing [key pain point]"

## Belief Stage 2: Understanding the Depth of the Problem
**Belief needed:** "This problem is bigger and more expensive than I thought."
**Starting belief:** They think the problem is minor or something they can manage manually.
**Content angle:** Reveal hidden costs, missed opportunities, or compounding effects.
**Example topic title:** "The true cost of [pain point] that nobody talks about"

## Belief Stage 3: Solution Category Awareness
**Belief needed:** "A structured solution to this problem exists."
**Starting belief:** They don't know what category of solution would help, or think they have to figure it out themselves.
**Content angle:** Introduce the solution category (not the product yet) as a legitimate, proven approach.
**Example topic title:** "How [solution category] has changed the way people solve [problem]"

## Belief Stage 4: Overcoming Skepticism
**Belief needed:** "Not all solutions in this category are risky or fraudulent."
**Starting belief:** They've heard of bad actors, failures, or scams in this space.
**Content angle:** Differentiate legitimate solutions from bad ones. Show what to look for.
**Example topic title:** "What separates trustworthy [solution category] from the rest"

## Belief Stage 5: Product Differentiation
**Belief needed:** "[Product] is the right choice within this solution category."
**Starting belief:** They see this product as just one of many options.
**Content angle:** Highlight specific, verifiable differentiators. Show what makes this product uniquely suited.
**Example topic title:** "Why [product] approaches [key differentiator] differently"

## Belief Stage 6: Trust Through Proof
**Belief needed:** "I can verify that this product does what it claims."
**Starting belief:** They want to trust it but need evidence, not just claims.
**Content angle:** On-chain proof, transparent data, real metrics, audit results, or third-party validation.
**Example topic title:** "Here's what you can verify about [product] right now"

## Belief Stage 7: Action Readiness
**Belief needed:** "I'm ready to try this, and I know how to start safely."
**Starting belief:** They're convinced but uncertain about the first step or afraid of making a mistake.
**Content angle:** Lower the barrier. Show the minimum viable first step. Reduce risk perception.
**Example topic title:** "The safest way to take your first step with [product]"
