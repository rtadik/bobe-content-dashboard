# BoBe Content Guidelines

Reference for the content-generator skill. All generated content must align with these principles.

---

## Brand Voice

**Transparent. Calm. Confident. Educational.**

BoBe speaks like a knowledgeable friend who actually trades — not a hype account or a finance bro. The tone is:

- Direct and honest about how the platform works
- Realistic about returns (never guaranteed)
- Reassuring without being salesy
- Smart without being condescending

---

## Messaging Pillars

Every piece of content should connect to one or more of these:

### 1. Automation
BoBe removes the emotional, time-consuming parts of trading.
> "You set the strategy. BoBe executes it — 24/7, without emotion."

### 2. Transparency
On-chain visibility, audited smart contracts, nothing hidden.
> "Every trade, every distribution — visible on-chain."

### 3. Risk Management
Spot only, no leverage, structured strategies.
> "Just spot. No leverage. No surprises."

### 4. Accessibility
For retail users who want exposure without needing to be expert traders.
> "You don't need to know how to trade. You need to know what you want."

---

## Twitter / X Guidelines

### Single Post (≤280 chars)
- Open with a hook or relatable pain point
- One clear insight or value prop
- Optional: 1 CTA (soft — "learn more", "see how it works")
- Max 2–3 hashtags: `#DeFi` `#CryptoTrading` `#TradingBot`
- No price predictions, no "x% guaranteed"

**Example:**
> Most traders lose not because of bad analysis — but because they can't stick to the plan.
> Automation fixes that.
> #CryptoTrading #TradingBot

### Thread (3–5 tweets)
- Tweet 1: Hook / bold claim or question
- Tweets 2–3: Education / breakdown / insight
- Tweet 4: How BoBe connects
- Tweet 5: CTA — soft link or "follow for more"

---

## Telegram Guidelines

- Longer form (500–1500 chars ideal)
- More educational, explain the "why"
- Can include bullet points for clarity
- End with a question to drive engagement: "What's your current strategy — manual or automated?"
- Avoid walls of text — use line breaks generously

---

## What to Always Avoid

| Avoid | Why |
|-------|-----|
| "Guaranteed X% APY" | Regulatory risk + damages trust |
| "100x your money" | Pure hype, off-brand |
| Rocket / moon emojis | Signals meme culture, not credibility |
| Urgency tactics ("Last chance!") | Off-brand, trust-damaging |
| Competitor bashing | Unprofessional |
| Complex jargon without explanation | Alienates target ICP |

## Content Category Types

| Type | Use Case |
|------|----------|
