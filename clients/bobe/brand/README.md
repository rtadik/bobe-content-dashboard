## The BoBe Mascot

The mascot is the single most important brand element. All image prompts should describe it precisely:

> **3D clay/plastic chibi figurine, young Asian man, round thick-framed glasses (dark brown/black), short dark brown hair styled upward, wearing a white BoBe t-shirt, cute round face, chubby proportions typical of chibi style, high-quality 3D render**

The mascot appears in various costumes/scenarios (samurai, casual, etc.) but the core character — glasses, round face, BoBe shirt — is consistent.

## Visual Style Principles

### Backgrounds
- **Default:** Deep dark navy (#111B32 → #070A1B gradient)
- **Cyberpunk/vibrant:** Neon blues, purples, pinks with light trails
- **Outdoor/photorealistic:** Real-world settings (city streets, offices)

### Typography
- Bold, large, san-serif (all caps or sentence case)
- White on dark backgrounds
- Short punchy headlines: max 5–6 words
- Secondary text smaller, lighter weight

### Composition
- Mascot is usually right-aligned or center, taking ~40–60% of frame
- Headline left or top
- BoBe logo always top-left corner
- Charts/UI elements appear as floating glass panels behind mascot

### What to Avoid
- Rockets, moons, lambos — too "crypto bro"
- Crowded, busy compositions
- Stock photo human faces
- Generic blue gradients with no mascot

## Image Prompt Template

Use this as the base for all Nano Banana Pro image prompts:

```
[SCENARIO DESCRIPTION], featuring the BoBe mascot (3D clay chibi figurine, young Asian man with round dark glasses, short styled hair, white BoBe t-shirt), [BACKGROUND DESCRIPTION], [COLOR PALETTE], bold white headline text "[HEADLINE]" in top-left area, BoBe APP logo top-left corner, professional social media banner composition, high resolution, 16:9 aspect ratio
```

### Example:
```
BoBe mascot (3D clay chibi figurine, young Asian man with round dark glasses, white BoBe t-shirt) sitting confidently at a trading desk with multiple screens showing green charts, deep dark navy background (#111B32), blue gradient accents (#1589DC to #49ACF2), bold white headline "While you sleep." top-left, BoBe APP logo top-left corner, cinematic 3D render, social media banner, 16:9
```
