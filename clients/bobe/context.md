# BoBe — Client Context

## Organization Overview

BoBe is a Web3 fintech platform that enables users to "Bake" their crypto assets into AI-driven trading strategies that generate automated on-chain yield.

The platform is designed for retail crypto users who want structured, rules-based exposure to trading performance without manually trading, managing leverage, or reacting emotionally to market volatility.

BoBe's mission is to provide transparent, automated, and risk-managed yield generation through audited smart contracts and on-chain visibility.

---

## Products / Services

* AI-driven automated trading strategies (e.g., DCA, grid, structured bots)
* On-chain yield distribution in USDT
* User-friendly deposit and tracking interface
* Affiliate and referral reward system
* Ambassador-driven community growth
* Education-driven marketing funnel (quizzes, lead magnets, belief-building content)
* Performance transparency and audit credibility

---

## Target Audience (ICP)

* Retail crypto or new traders, or traders exhausted from losing
* Age 20-35 with $500-$20,000 in crypto
* Pain points: inconsistent trading results, emotional mistakes, lack of time, distrust of unrealistic APY claims

---

## Positioning

* Emphasizes automation, transparency, and risk management
* Avoids hype-driven messaging and performance guarantees
* Growth strategy relies on content, ambassadors, KOL collaborations, and funnel-based acquisition rather than pure paid ads
* Competes in a crowded yield and trading automation market where trust and differentiation are critical
